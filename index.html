WHAT NUDGE IS

Two people keep each other honest. Each of you tracks habits to build (do it every day) and habits to break (stay clean every day), as daily yes/no streaks with a progress bar toward a target. Your partner gets your daily and weekly summaries on Telegram, and a nudge the moment you press About to relapse, one tap, nothing to type.

There is no account and no server. Everything you type is encrypted in this browser with your passkey. The only thing that ever leaves the browser is a message you asked to send, straight to Telegram.

1 · SET UP THE APP

  1. Open this page and set a passkey. It encrypts your data; there is no "forgot passkey", so keep it somewhere safe.
  2. Tick Stay unlocked on this device if this is your own phone, so the app opens without asking. Otherwise it locks when you close it.
  3. Enter your first name: that is how your partner will see you in messages.
  4. Add your habits on the Habits tab. Pick an emoji, Build or Break, and a target in days (30, 66, 90, 365).
  5. Every day, open Today and mark each habit. Build: did it or not. Break: clean or slipped.
  6. Take a backup now and then (⚙ → Backup). The backup file is the encrypted vault; it opens with the same passkey on any device.

2 · SAVE IT TO YOUR HOME SCREEN

Nudge is a web app. Saved to the Home Screen it opens full-screen like any app, works offline, and keeps its data (as long as you don't clear the browser's site data).

iPhone / iPad (Safari)Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow), scroll, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add. Open Nudge from the new icon from now on: Safari and the Home Screen app keep separate data.
Android (Chrome)Tap the ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen (or Install app) → Install.
Desktop (Chrome / Edge)Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or menu → Install Nudge.

3 · CONNECT TELEGRAM

Each of you makes your own bot. Your bot is your messenger: your app sends your partner's Telegram the summaries and nudges through it. Your partner's app does the same through their bot. Nobody shares a token, nothing passes through a third server, and you keep chatting with each other directly in Telegram as usual.

YOU YOUR PARTNER Nudge app ──(your bot)──▶ Telegram: "Sam's check-in 📅 …" Telegram ◀──(their bot)── Nudge app You two talk in your normal Telegram chat.

Create your bot (2 minutes, each of you):

  1. In Telegram, open @BotFather and send /newbot.
  2. Give it a name (e.g. Sam's Nudge) and a username ending in bot (e.g. sam_nudge_bot).
  3. BotFather replies with a token like 123456789:AAH…. Copy it.
  4. In Nudge → Partner, paste the token and press Save & check token. The app asks Telegram for the bot's name to confirm it works.
  5. Send your bot's username to your partner. Your partner opens it in Telegram and presses Start.
  6. Back in Nudge, press Find my partner: the app asks your bot who has messaged it and lists the chats. Pick your partner.
  7. Press Send a test. Your partner should receive it within a second.
  8. Your partner repeats the same steps with their own bot, and you press Start on theirs.
TipOptional: in BotFather, /setuserpic gives the bot a picture and /setdescription a line like "Sam's accountability nudges". Keep the token to yourself: anyone with it can send messages as that bot.

4 · WHAT YOUR PARTNER RECEIVES

  1. Daily summary: once you have marked every habit for the day (automatic if enabled, or the button on Today).
  2. Weekly summary: days done per habit, streaks and bests for the week that ended (automatic on your first visit in a new week, or the button).
  3. Relapse nudge: the red button, immediately, with the habit at stake and its streak. No typing: with one habit it sends on the spot, with several you tap the one at stake. Agree with your partner what they do when it arrives: call, reply, meet.

5 · TROUBLESHOOTING

  1. "Unauthorized" when saving the token: the token was copied wrong or revoked. Ask BotFather for /token again.
  2. Find my partner finds nobody: your partner must message the bot first (press Start). Telegram only keeps those messages for 24 hours, so press Find soon after.
  3. "Forbidden: bot was blocked by the user": your partner blocked or deleted the bot chat; they need to Start it again.
  4. Nothing arrives: check the Sent log on the Partner tab for the error text, and the phone has internet. Messages are sent only while the app is open and you act.
  5. Lost passkey: there is no recovery. Clear the browser's site data (or ⚙ → Reset) and set up again from a backup you can open, or from scratch.

6 · PRIVACY MODEL

The page is static (it is served from GitHub Pages and contains nothing of yours). Your vault is one encrypted blob in this browser's storage; the key comes from your passkey and is never stored (unless you choose Stay unlocked, which keeps a non-extractable copy of the derived key in this browser only). Telegram sees exactly the messages you send and nothing else. The Sent log is in the vault too.
